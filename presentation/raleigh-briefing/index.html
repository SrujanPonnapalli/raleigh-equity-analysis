The calculation.

Appendix A
Outcome

A request counts as resolved within the window when its recorded resolution timestamp falls within 90 or 180 calendar days of submission.

All eligible submissions stay in the denominator. Missing resolution is counted as not resolved within the window.

Comparable task / month

Service + request type (including unspecified) × local submission month. Compare only retained strata represented on both sides.

Direct standardization
Rg = Σs ws · rgs

r = group resolution proportion in a stratum
w = pooled stratum cases / all retained cases

Both groups receive the same weights.
Gap = 100 × (focal rate − reference rate).

Weights and retained samples can differ between comparisons. Adjusted rates are weighted percentages, not simple resolved / total fractions.

Descriptive exploratory comparisons. No causal identification, adjusted significance claims, or confidence intervals.
